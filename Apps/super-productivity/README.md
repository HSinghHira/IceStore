# Super Productivity

Super Productivity is a to-do list made for focused work. Plan your day, track the time you spend on each task, take breaks with the Pomodoro timer, and pull in issues from Jira, GitHub, GitLab and more.

## Features

- Tasks with sub-tasks, notes, attachments, tags and projects
- Time tracking, timeboxing and work-day summaries
- Pomodoro timer and break reminders
- Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, OpenProject and CalDAV integrations
- Sync between devices with WebDAV or Dropbox, and full offline use

## How to use

1. Open Super Productivity and add your first task.
2. Start the timer on a task while you work.
3. To use it on several devices, set up WebDAV sync in Settings (for example with a WebDAV app on your ZimaOS).

## Before you install

Super Productivity runs in your browser and keeps your tasks in that browser's storage; nothing is saved on the ZimaOS and there is no login. To keep a copy or use it on other devices, turn on sync (WebDAV, Dropbox or a local file) in Settings. The desktop and phone apps can sync with the same WebDAV folder.

# OpenSpeedTest

OpenSpeedTest measures the download and upload speed, ping and jitter between any device and your ZimaOS, right in the browser. Use it to check your Wi-Fi, cables and switches, with no app to install.

## Features

- Download, upload, ping and jitter in any browser
- Tests your home network, not your internet connection
- No app, no account and no data kept
- Works on phones, tablets, computers and smart TVs

## How to use

1. Open OpenSpeedTest on the device to test and press Start.

## Before you install

OpenSpeedTest measures the speed between the device that opens it and your ZimaOS, so it shows your home network (Wi-Fi, cables, switches), not your internet speed. It keeps no data and has no login.
